* What were you experimenting with with your prototype?

In terms of my prototype, i was attempting to implement very basic ideas so that I could get used the programming. The first thing that I knew that I wanted to implement was a collecting system, as in collecting coins and updating the score UI. Once I figured that out, my goal was to implement a grab and drop system. This took the longest,With me having to configureThe camera, Mouse, and an empty game object representing the hand so that the objects could be picked up

When it came to my second and final draft of my prototype, I wanted to add more game mechanics, such as moving platforms, falling traps, and flashlights. I spent a lot of time trying to figure out how to program it so that when a flashlight beam hits certain objects, they will appear.

What did you learn?

I learned a lot in terms of different ways to create the things I wanted, and I also learned to save every few minutes because I have a habit of changing one thing and breaking the entire game.
